Terms of Use – Actual Budget Family AI

Last updated: 24 October 2026

Purpose and eligibility

Actual Budget Family AI is a personal, non-commercial budgeting tool operated privately for the owner and authorised family members. It is not offered to the public.

Bank data access

Connecting a bank account requires the account holder's explicit authorisation through the bank and Enable Banking. The connection is for reading account information and importing transactions; the application does not initiate payments, transfers or other banking operations.

Responsibilities

Users must protect their access credentials and ensure they are authorised to connect the accounts they use. Budget figures and synchronised data should be reviewed against official bank records before important decisions.

Availability and third parties

The service is provided for personal use without any guarantee of uninterrupted availability or error-free bank synchronisation. Actual Budget, Enable Banking, the selected bank, OVHcloud and Google Drive have their own applicable terms and policies.

Changes and termination

The owner may update or discontinue the private service. Bank access can be revoked by the account holder using the bank's consent controls or relevant connection settings.

Contact

For questions, contact the application owner using the data-protection contact email provided in the Enable Banking application registration.
