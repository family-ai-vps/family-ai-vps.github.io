Privacy Notice – Actual Budget Family AI

Last updated: 24 October 2026

Actual Budget Family AI is a private, non-commercial personal and family budgeting application, not a public financial service.

Data and purposes

The application may process bank account identifiers, balances, transaction descriptions, dates and amounts, and budgeting categories to display and organise the budget and synchronise authorised bank transactions. No payment initiation is provided.

Bank connections

When explicitly authorised by the account holder, Enable Banking facilitates access to account information from the selected bank. The bank and Enable Banking process data for authentication and account-information access under their own applicable notices. Bank consent can be withdrawn through the bank or the relevant connection controls.

Hosting and protection

The self-hosted Actual Budget service runs on a private OVHcloud server located in Strasbourg, France. Access is restricted using a private network and authentication. Encrypted backup archives are stored in the owner's Google Drive account; Google acts as a storage provider for those encrypted archives. Data handling by external bank-connection providers may involve other locations and rules.

Access, retention and deletion

Use is restricted to authorised family members. Financial data is retained while needed for personal budgeting and backups according to the owner's retention settings. The account holder may request correction or removal from the application owner. Copies in retained backups may remain until backup expiration.

Contact

For privacy enquiries, contact the application owner using the data-protection contact email provided in the Enable Banking application registration.
