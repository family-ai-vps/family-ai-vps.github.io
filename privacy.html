Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

Family AI Backups is a personal, non-commercial application used exclusively by its owner to back up a private family server.

Google Drive access

The application uses the Google Drive application data folder to upload, list, retrieve and manage encrypted backup files. It does not require access to other personal files in Google Drive.

Data processing

Backup data is encrypted locally before upload. The application does not use backup content for advertising, analytics or profiling.

Data sharing

No personal data is sold. Encrypted backups are stored in the owner's Google Drive account and processed by Google according to its applicable terms.

Retention and deletion

Backup files are retained according to the owner's backup settings. The owner can delete them using the backup application and its storage tools.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the application owner through the support email displayed on the Google OAuth consent screen.
